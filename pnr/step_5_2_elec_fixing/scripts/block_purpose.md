# Step 5_2 — Electrical Fix

This block addresses the electrical design-rule violations that appeared after ECO cell legalization.

### Goal

- Analyze **Max Capacitance** and **Max Transition** violations.
- Identify their root causes and affected nets/cells.
- Apply the required ECO fixes.
- Re-legalize the modified cells.
- Verify that the violations are resolved without reintroducing **hold timing violations**.
